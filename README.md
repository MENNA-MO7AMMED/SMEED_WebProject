Project Description📋
SMEED is a modern, all-in-one Student Management Platform designed to empower students to take control of their academic, personal, and financial lives. With a focus on usability, security, and actionable insights, SMEED provides a seamless experience for students to manage their schedules, health, finances, worship routines, and more—all in one place. 🚀

Key Features:
------------

✅User Authentication & Security
Sign Up & Login: 🔑
Secure registration and login with validation for username, email, phone, and national ID. 
Password Management: Passwords are securely hashed; users can reset their password via email verification.
Email Verification: OTP-based email verification for added security.
Session Management: Persistent login with local storage and session handling.

✅Personal DashboardProfile Management: 📑
View and update personal information, including uploading a profile photo.
Quick Stats: At-a-glance overview of appointments, health, worship, academic, and financial stats.
Customizable Experience: Language and theme toggling for accessibility and personalization.

✅Appointments & Task ManagementSmart Calendar: 🗓️
Visualize and manage all tasks and appointments in a calendar view.
Kanban Board: Organize tasks by status (To Do, In Progress, Completed) for efficient workflow.
Task Analytics: Detailed statistics and productivity insights, including completion rates and overdue tasks.
PDF Export: Generate professional, branded PDF reports of your tasks and appointments.
Reminders: Enable reminders for important tasks and events.

✅Financial ManagementMulti-Account Support: 💵💰
Create and manage up to 4 financial accounts in different currencies (EGP, USD, EUR, GBP).
Expense Tracking: Log expenses by category (food, transport, housing, entertainment, education, other) and account.
Analytics & Reports: CSV reports, and analyze expenses
Transfers & Deposits: Transfer money between accounts and deposit funds securely.
Savings Goals: Set, track, and manage savings goals with daily progress and completion tracking.

✅Health & WellnessHealth Profile: 
Record and update health metrics such as  height, weight, and fitness goals.
BMI Calculation
Water & Exercise Recommendations: Personalized daily water intake and exercise suggestions based on profile.
Health Plans: Start and track progress on custom health plans.
PDF/Excel Reports: Export health data and progress for any period.

✅Worship & Spiritual TrackingReligious Preferences: 🛐
Set up and manage religious preferences (Islam, Christianity, Other).
Prayer Times: Automatic calculation and display of daily prayer times based on location and method.
Prayer Completion Tracking: Mark prayers as completed and track daily/weekly/monthly progress.
Reports: Generate PDF/Excel reports of worship activity and completion rates.

✅Academic & PlansAcademic Overview:
Track courses, assignments, exams, mode improver, Chat community between students
Plans & Subscriptions: View and select from different subscription plans (Free, Pro, Enterprise) with feature comparison and Payment Integration

✅Modern UI/UXResponsive Design:
Fully responsive and mobile-friendly interface.
- Dark/Light Mode
- Animated Transitions
- Accessibility RTL/LTR language support

✅Technology StackFrontend: 
- React, Framer Motion for animations, Lucide icons, custom UI components.
- Backend: Node.js with Express, MySQL for data storage, bcrypt for password hashing, nodemailer for emails.
- PDF/CSV Generation: jsPDF and html2canvas for PDF, native CSV export

Why SMEED❓🤔
SMEED is more than just a student planner—it’s a holistic life management tool. Whether you want to ace your exams, stay healthy, manage your money, or keep up with your spiritual practices, SMEED brings everything together in a single, secure, and beautifully designed platform.🧨
